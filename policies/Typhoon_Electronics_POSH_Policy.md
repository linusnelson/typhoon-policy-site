## Preamble

This Policy ("**Policy**") forms part of the Human Resources Policy Manual of Typhoon Electronics Solutions ("**the Company**", "**Typhoon Electronics**", "**Employer**") and shall apply with effect from the Effective Date stated above. This Policy is issued under the authority of the Chief Executive Officer and supersedes all prior policies, circulars, oral understandings, or established practices on the matters covered herein.

This Policy is issued in discharge of the Company's obligations under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 ("**the Act**") and the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Rules, 2013 ("**the Rules**"), and gives effect to the fundamental rights guaranteed under Articles 14, 15, 19(1)(g) and 21 of the Constitution of India.

Sexual harassment at the workplace is a violation of the dignity, equality, and liberty of the person. The Company maintains a position of **zero tolerance** in respect of such conduct.

Where any provision of this Policy is, or becomes, inconsistent with the Act or the Rules, the Act and the Rules shall prevail, and this Policy shall be read as modified to that extent. The Company reserves the right to amend, modify, or rescind this Policy, or any part thereof, at any time, subject always to the statutory minimum. Any such modification shall be communicated through an official circular issued by the Human Resources function or the Chief Executive Officer.

## Scope and Applicability

This Policy shall apply to:

1. All persons employed by Typhoon Electronics on a full-time basis, whether on probation or confirmed, and irrespective of designation, location, or length of service;
2. Trainees, apprentices, interns, and persons engaged on a temporary, ad-hoc, or daily-wage basis, whether engaged directly or through an agent;
3. Contractors, consultants, vendors' personnel, and probationers, in respect of their conduct at the Workplace or in connection with Company business;
4. Any woman, of any age, whether employed or not, who alleges to have been subjected to any act of sexual harassment at the Workplace, including visitors, customers' personnel, and job applicants.

Each individual to whom this Policy applies is hereinafter referred to as an "**Employee**", unless the context requires otherwise.

## 1.1 Purpose

1.1.1 This Policy is issued to:

(a) Prevent and prohibit sexual harassment of women at the Workplace;
(b) Provide a mechanism for the redressal of complaints of sexual harassment that is prompt, impartial, confidential, and consistent with the principles of natural justice;
(c) Discharge the Company's statutory duties under Section 19 of the Act;
(d) Promote a working environment in which every person is treated with dignity and respect.

1.1.2 This Policy is to be read together with the Code of Conduct and the Equal Opportunity Policy. Complaints of discrimination, or of harassment that is not sexual in nature, fall to be dealt with under the Equal Opportunity Policy and the Code of Conduct, and not under this Policy.

## 1.2 Zero Tolerance and Gender-Neutral Commitment

1.2.1 The Company maintains **zero tolerance** towards sexual harassment. Any Employee found to have committed sexual harassment shall be liable to disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, without prejudice to any criminal or civil liability arising under law.

1.2.2 The statutory machinery of the Act protects women. The Company nevertheless affirms that **no person of any gender or sexual orientation shall be subjected to sexual harassment at the Workplace.** A complaint of sexual harassment made by a person who is not an Aggrieved Woman within the meaning of the Act shall be inquired into by the Internal Committee under the Code of Conduct, applying the same standards of confidentiality, natural justice, protection from retaliation, and timeliness prescribed by this Policy, save that the statutory remedies available exclusively under the Act (including appeal under Section 18 of the Act) shall not apply to such a complaint as a matter of right.

## 1.3 What Constitutes Sexual Harassment

1.3.1 **Sexual Harassment** includes any one or more of the following unwelcome acts or behaviour, whether directly or by implication (Section 2(n) of the Act):

(a) Physical contact and advances;
(b) A demand or request for sexual favours;
(c) Making sexually coloured remarks;
(d) Showing pornography;
(e) Any other unwelcome physical, verbal, or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature.

1.3.2 Without limiting clause 1.3.1, the following circumstances, if they occur or are present in relation to or connected with any act or behaviour of sexual harassment, may amount to sexual harassment (Section 3(2) of the Act):

(a) Implied or explicit promise of preferential treatment in employment;
(b) Implied or explicit threat of detrimental treatment in employment;
(c) Implied or explicit threat about the person's present or future employment status;
(d) Interference with work, or the creation of an intimidating, offensive, or hostile work environment;
(e) Humiliating treatment likely to affect health or safety.

1.3.3 By way of illustration and not of limitation, the following conduct is prohibited: unwelcome touching, patting, pinching, or brushing against the body; staring or leering; sexually suggestive comments, jokes, gestures, or sounds; questions or comments about a person's body, appearance, sexual conduct, or private life; circulation, display, or storage of sexually explicit material by any medium, including electronic mail, instant messaging, and social media; persistent unwanted invitations of a romantic or sexual nature; stalking, whether physical or electronic; and any threat or reprisal following the refusal of a sexual advance.

1.3.4 **The test of unwelcomeness is that of the recipient.** Whether conduct is unwelcome is to be assessed from the standpoint of the person subjected to it, and not by reference to the intention of the person committing it. The absence of protest at the time does not signify consent or welcomeness.

1.3.5 Conduct that is a legitimate and bona fide exercise of managerial or supervisory authority — including performance counselling, allocation of work, appraisal, or disciplinary action carried out in good faith — does not constitute sexual harassment.

## 1.4 Meaning of "Workplace"

1.4.1 For the purposes of this Policy, **Workplace** is to be construed widely and includes (Section 2(o) of the Act):

(a) All premises, offices, factories, stores, and units of the Company, including the registered office at Maraimalai Nagar and every branch, site, or location of the Company;
(b) Any place visited by an Employee arising out of or during the course of employment, including a client's or vendor's premises, a site of a scheduled or ad-hoc client visit, and any training venue, conference, seminar, or event organised by or attended on behalf of the Company;
(c) Transportation provided by the Company, or used for the purpose of undertaking a journey arising out of employment, including travel undertaken under the Travel Policy;
(d) Any accommodation provided by the Company in connection with travel or deputation;
(e) Company-hosted or Company-sponsored social events, team outings, and celebrations, whether held on or off Company premises;
(f) The **virtual or digital workplace**, including electronic mail, messaging platforms, video conferences, the Employee Portal, and any other Company system or channel, and communications made through personal devices or accounts where the communication arises out of or in the course of employment;
(g) The residence of an Employee, in respect of any period during which the Employee is recorded as working from home under the Attendance & Leave Policy.

1.4.2 Conduct occurring outside working hours may nevertheless fall within this Policy where it has a sufficient nexus to the employment relationship or affects the Workplace.

## 1.5 Internal Committee

1.5.1 In compliance with Section 4 of the Act, the Company shall constitute an **Internal Committee** at the Workplace by written order of the Employer. The names, designations, and contact particulars of the members shall be notified by a separate circular in the form of **Annexure A**, which shall be displayed at conspicuous places at the Workplace and published on the Employee Portal, and which shall form part of this Policy without requiring its re-issue. Any subsequent change in the composition of the Internal Committee shall likewise be notified by circular in the same form.

1.5.2 The Internal Committee shall comprise:

(a) A **Presiding Officer**, being a woman employed at a senior level at the Workplace; and where a senior-level woman employee is not available, a woman employed at a senior level at another office or unit of the Company, or, if that too is not available, a woman nominated from another workplace or organisation in accordance with the proviso to Section 4(2)(a) of the Act;
(b) Not less than **two Members** from amongst Employees, preferably committed to the cause of women, or who have had experience in social work, or who possess legal knowledge;
(c) One **External Member** from amongst non-governmental organisations or associations committed to the cause of women, or a person familiar with issues relating to sexual harassment.

1.5.3 **Not less than one-half of the total members of the Internal Committee shall be women.**

1.5.4 The Presiding Officer and every Member shall hold office for a term not exceeding **three years** from the date of nomination, and shall be eligible for renomination.

1.5.5 The External Member shall be paid such fees or allowances for holding proceedings of the Internal Committee as are prescribed by Rule 4 of the Rules, together with travelling allowance, and the Company shall bear the same.

1.5.6 The Presiding Officer or any Member shall be removed from the Internal Committee, and the vacancy filled by fresh nomination, where such person (Section 4(5) of the Act):

(a) Contravenes the confidentiality obligation at Section 16 of the Act and clause 1.13;
(b) Has been convicted of an offence, or an inquiry into an offence under any law is pending against them;
(c) Has been found guilty in a disciplinary proceeding, or a disciplinary proceeding is pending against them;
(d) Has so abused their position as to render their continuance prejudicial to the public interest.

1.5.7 The Internal Committee shall meet not less than once each quarter, and otherwise as required for the disposal of complaints. **Three members, including the Presiding Officer, shall constitute the quorum** for any inquiry proceeding (Section 11(3) proviso and Rule 7(4) of the Rules).

1.5.8 Where the complaint is against the Presiding Officer or a Member, that person shall recuse themselves from all proceedings relating to the complaint, and the Employer shall nominate a substitute for the purposes of that inquiry. Where the complaint is against the Employer, the Complainant may make the complaint directly to the Local Committee of the district.

1.5.9 **Transitional provision.** This Policy takes effect on the Effective Date, whether or not the Internal Committee has by then been notified under clause 1.5.1. Until the date on which Annexure A is notified:

(a) The prohibitions at clauses 1.2, 1.3, and 1.14 and the obligations of the Company at clause 1.6 apply in full, and any conduct in contravention of them remains misconduct under the Code of Conduct from the Effective Date;
(b) A complaint may be made to the **Chief Executive Officer**, or through the interim intake channel published on the Employee Portal, and shall be acknowledged in writing, recorded, and held in confidence in accordance with clause 1.13;
(c) A complaint so made shall be **placed before the Internal Committee immediately upon its notification**, and the limitation period at clause 1.7.2 shall be reckoned from the date of the incident (or of the last incident), not from the date of notification of the Internal Committee, so that no Complainant is prejudiced by the interim period;
(d) A Complainant may at any time, and irrespective of this clause, make her complaint to the Local Committee of the district, file through the SHe-Box portal, or pursue any remedy in criminal or civil law, and the Company shall render the assistance required by clause 1.6.1(g);
(e) The Employer shall consider and, where appropriate, grant interim relief of the kind described at clause 1.10.1 pending constitution of the Internal Committee.

1.5.10 This transitional clause does not dilute the Company's obligation under Section 4 of the Act to constitute the Internal Committee, and the Internal Committee shall be notified at the earliest and in any event **within ninety days of the Effective Date**.

## 1.6 Duties of the Employer

1.6.1 In discharge of Section 19 of the Act, the Company shall:

(a) Provide a safe working environment, including safety from persons with whom an Employee comes into contact at the Workplace;
(b) Display at conspicuous places at the Workplace the **penal consequences of sexual harassment** and the composition and contact particulars of the Internal Committee;
(c) Organise **awareness workshops and orientation programmes** for Employees at regular intervals, and capacity-building and skill-development programmes for members of the Internal Committee;
(d) Provide the Internal Committee with such facilities as are necessary for dealing with a complaint and conducting an inquiry;
(e) Assist in securing the attendance of the Respondent and witnesses before the Internal Committee;
(f) Make available to the Internal Committee such information as it may require in relation to the complaint;
(g) Provide assistance to the Complainant if she chooses to file a complaint under the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023** (which, with effect from 1 July 2024, replaced the Indian Penal Code, 1860) or under any other law;
(h) Cause to initiate action, under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 or any other law, against the perpetrator, or, where the Aggrieved Woman so desires, where the perpetrator is not an Employee, in the workplace at which the incident of sexual harassment took place;
(i) Treat sexual harassment as **misconduct** under the Code of Conduct and initiate action for such misconduct;
(j) Monitor the timely submission of reports by the Internal Committee.

1.6.2 Every Employee shall be required to read this Policy and to record acknowledgement of the same on the Employee Portal. Acknowledgement shall be a condition of continued engagement and shall be recorded afresh upon each material revision of this Policy.

1.6.3 New Employees shall be taken through this Policy as part of induction, and the composition of the Internal Committee shall be communicated to them at that time.

## 1.7 Making a Complaint

1.7.1 An Aggrieved Woman may make a complaint of sexual harassment **in writing** to the Internal Committee, addressed to the Presiding Officer, at the contact particulars notified under clause 1.5.1 in the form of Annexure A. The complaint may be delivered by hand, by post, or by electronic mail. Pending notification of the Internal Committee, a complaint may be made through the interim channel at clause 1.5.9(b).

1.7.2 The complaint shall be made within a period of **three months from the date of the incident**, and, in the case of a series of incidents, within a period of three months from the date of the last incident. The Internal Committee may, for reasons to be recorded in writing, **extend the time limit by a further period not exceeding three months**, if it is satisfied that circumstances were such as prevented the Complainant from filing a complaint within the said period.

1.7.3 **Six copies** of the complaint, together with supporting documents and the names and addresses of witnesses, shall be submitted in accordance with Rule 7(1) of the Rules. Where the Complainant is unable to make the complaint in writing, the Presiding Officer or any Member shall render all reasonable assistance to her for making the complaint in writing.

1.7.4 Where the Aggrieved Woman is unable to make a complaint on account of physical or mental incapacity, death, or otherwise, a complaint may be made by the persons specified in Rule 6 of the Rules, including her relative or friend, a co-worker, an officer of the National or State Commission for Women, a person having knowledge of the incident with her written consent, or, in the event of her death, her legal heir.

1.7.5 A complaint may additionally, or in the alternative, be filed through the **SHe-Box** portal maintained by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. A complaint so filed shall be dealt with by the Internal Committee in the same manner as a complaint made directly to it.

1.7.6 The making of a complaint under this Policy does not preclude the Complainant from pursuing any remedy available in criminal or civil law, and the Company shall render assistance under clause 1.6.1(g).

## 1.8 Conciliation

1.8.1 Before initiating an inquiry, the Internal Committee may, **at the request of the Aggrieved Woman and not otherwise**, take steps to settle the matter between her and the Respondent through conciliation (Section 10 of the Act).

1.8.2 **No monetary settlement shall be made the basis of conciliation.**

1.8.3 Where a settlement is arrived at, the Internal Committee shall record the settlement and forward it to the Employer for taking action as specified in the recommendation. Copies of the settlement shall be provided to the Aggrieved Woman and the Respondent.

1.8.4 Where a settlement is arrived at under this clause, **no further inquiry shall be conducted** by the Internal Committee in respect of the same complaint. Where the Aggrieved Woman informs the Internal Committee that any term of the settlement has not been complied with, the Internal Committee shall proceed to conduct an inquiry, or forward the complaint to the police, as the case may be.

## 1.9 Inquiry

1.9.1 Where conciliation is not requested, or does not result in a settlement, the Internal Committee shall proceed to make an inquiry into the complaint in accordance with the Act, the Rules, and the Code of Conduct.

1.9.2 The Internal Committee shall, within **seven working days** of receipt of the complaint, send one copy of the complaint to the Respondent (Rule 7(2) of the Rules).

1.9.3 The Respondent shall file a reply to the complaint, together with a list of documents and the names and addresses of witnesses, within a period not exceeding **ten working days** from the date of receipt of the complaint (Rule 7(3) of the Rules).

1.9.4 The inquiry shall be conducted in accordance with the **principles of natural justice**. Both parties shall be given a fair opportunity to be heard, to present documents and witnesses, and to respond to the material relied upon against them.

1.9.5 **Neither party shall be permitted to bring a legal practitioner to represent them** in any proceeding before the Internal Committee (Rule 7(6) of the Rules).

1.9.6 Where the Complainant or the Respondent fails, without sufficient cause, to present themselves for **three consecutive hearings** convened by the Presiding Officer, the Internal Committee may, after giving fifteen days' written notice, terminate the proceedings or give an ex-parte decision, provided that no such order shall be passed without giving the party a notice in writing (Rule 7(4) of the Rules).

1.9.7 The Internal Committee shall have, for the purpose of the inquiry, the powers vested in a civil court under the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 in respect of summoning and enforcing the attendance of any person and examining them on oath, and requiring the discovery and production of documents (Section 11(3) of the Act).

1.9.8 **The inquiry shall be completed within a period of ninety days** from the date of receipt of the complaint (Section 11(4) of the Act).

1.9.9 The standard of proof shall be the **preponderance of probabilities**, being the standard applicable to a domestic inquiry, and not the criminal standard of proof beyond reasonable doubt.

## 1.10 Interim Relief During Pendency of Inquiry

1.10.1 During the pendency of an inquiry, on a written request made by the Aggrieved Woman, the Internal Committee may recommend to the Employer any of the following (Section 12 of the Act and Rule 8 of the Rules):

(a) Transfer of the Aggrieved Woman or the Respondent to any other workplace;
(b) Grant of **leave to the Aggrieved Woman of up to three months**, which shall be **in addition to the leave she would otherwise be entitled to** under the Attendance & Leave Policy, and which shall not be deducted from any leave balance;
(c) Restraint of the Respondent from reporting on the work performance of the Aggrieved Woman, or from writing her confidential report, and assignment of that function to another officer;
(d) Restraint of the Respondent from any supervisory, appraisal, or reporting relationship with the Aggrieved Woman;
(e) Grant of such other relief to the Aggrieved Woman as may be appropriate.

1.10.2 The Employer shall implement the recommendation made under clause 1.10.1 and send a report of such implementation to the Internal Committee.

## 1.11 Findings, Report, and Action

1.11.1 The Internal Committee shall, within **ten days** of the completion of the inquiry, provide a report of its findings to the Employer, and the report shall be made available to both parties (Section 13(1) of the Act).

1.11.2 **Where the allegation is not proved**, the Internal Committee shall recommend to the Employer that no action is required to be taken in the matter.

1.11.3 **Where the allegation is proved**, the Internal Committee shall recommend to the Employer:

(a) That action be taken for sexual harassment as **misconduct** in accordance with the Code of Conduct, which may include a written apology, warning, reprimand or censure, withholding of promotion, withholding of pay rise or increment, termination of employment, or a direction to undergo counselling or to carry out community service; and
(b) That such sum as it may determine be **deducted from the salary or wages of the Respondent** and paid to the Aggrieved Woman or to her legal heirs, and where such deduction is not possible on account of the Respondent being absent from duty or the cessation of employment, that the Respondent pay such sum to the Aggrieved Woman (Section 13(3)(ii) of the Act).

1.11.4 In determining the sum to be paid under clause 1.11.3(b), the Internal Committee shall have regard to (Section 15 of the Act): the mental trauma, pain, suffering, and emotional distress caused; the loss in career opportunity; medical expenses incurred for physical or psychiatric treatment; the income and financial status of the Respondent; and the feasibility of payment in a lump sum or in instalments.

1.11.5 **The Employer shall act upon the recommendation within sixty days** of receipt of the report (Section 13(4) of the Act).

1.11.6 Where the Respondent is not an Employee — including a contractor's or vendor's personnel, a customer's personnel, or a visitor — the Internal Committee shall recommend such action as is available to the Company, including prohibition of entry to the Workplace, escalation to the Respondent's employer, suspension or termination of the commercial engagement, and assistance to the Aggrieved Woman in pursuing remedies in law.

## 1.12 Appeal

1.12.1 Any person aggrieved by the recommendations of the Internal Committee, or by the non-implementation of such recommendations, may prefer an **appeal** to the appellate authority notified under Section 2(a) of the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, within a period of **ninety days** of the recommendation (Section 18 of the Act and Rule 11 of the Rules).

## 1.13 Confidentiality

1.13.1 Notwithstanding anything contained in the Right to Information Act, 2005, the contents of the complaint, the identity and addresses of the Aggrieved Woman, the Respondent, and the witnesses, any information relating to conciliation and inquiry proceedings, the recommendations of the Internal Committee, and the action taken by the Employer **shall not be published, communicated, or made known to the public, press, or media in any manner** (Section 16 of the Act).

1.13.2 Information may be disseminated regarding the justice secured to any victim of sexual harassment under the Act, without disclosing the name, address, identity, or any other particulars calculated to lead to the identification of the Aggrieved Woman or the witnesses.

1.13.3 Any person who contravenes clause 1.13.1 shall be liable to a **penalty of ₹5,000** recoverable in accordance with Section 17 of the Act and Rule 12 of the Rules, and to disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct.

1.13.4 Records of complaints and inquiries shall be maintained by the Presiding Officer in a secure and access-controlled manner, separately from ordinary personnel records, and shall be accessible only to members of the Internal Committee and to such persons as are required to implement its recommendations.

## 1.14 Protection Against Retaliation

1.14.1 **No adverse action shall be taken against any person** by reason of their having made a complaint under this Policy, having given evidence or assistance in an inquiry, or having supported another person in doing so.

1.14.2 Retaliation includes dismissal, demotion, adverse appraisal, denial of leave or opportunity, transfer to the detriment of the person, exclusion, ostracism, or any other detriment.

1.14.3 Retaliation shall itself constitute **gross misconduct** under the Code of Conduct and shall be dealt with accordingly, whether or not the underlying complaint is ultimately substantiated.

## 1.15 False or Malicious Complaints

1.15.1 Where the Internal Committee arrives at the conclusion that the allegation against the Respondent is **malicious**, or that the Aggrieved Woman or any other person making the complaint has made the complaint knowing it to be false, or has produced any forged or misleading document, it may recommend action against the person who made the complaint, in accordance with the Code of Conduct (Section 14(1) of the Act).

1.15.2 **Mere inability to substantiate a complaint, or the failure to provide adequate proof, shall not attract action under clause 1.15.1.** A finding of malicious intent must be arrived at only after an inquiry into that specific question, conducted in accordance with the procedure prescribed for the inquiry of a complaint, and after giving the person concerned an opportunity of being heard.

1.15.3 Where a witness is found to have given false evidence or produced a forged or misleading document, the Internal Committee may recommend action against that witness in the same manner (Section 14(2) of the Act).

## 1.16 Annual Report and Statutory Disclosure

1.16.1 The Internal Committee shall, in each calendar year, prepare an **annual report** and submit it to the Employer and to the District Officer, setting out (Section 21 of the Act and Rule 14 of the Rules):

(a) The number of complaints of sexual harassment received in the year;
(b) The number of complaints disposed of during the year;
(c) The number of cases pending for more than ninety days;
(d) The number of workshops or awareness programmes carried out;
(e) The nature of action taken by the Employer.

1.16.2 The Company shall include in its **Board's Report** the number of cases filed, if any, and their disposal, under the Act, as required by Section 22 of the Act and, where applicable, Rule 8(5)(x) of the Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014.

1.16.3 The Company shall provide the District Officer with such information as may be sought in relation to compliance with the Act.

## 1.17 Consequences of Non-Compliance by the Company

1.17.1 Failure by the Company to constitute an Internal Committee, or to comply with Sections 13, 14, 22 or any other provision of the Act or the Rules, attracts a **fine which may extend to ₹50,000**, and, upon a repeat contravention, twice the punishment, cancellation of licence or registration, or non-renewal or withdrawal of the same (Section 26 of the Act). Compliance with this Policy is accordingly a mandatory obligation of every person exercising managerial authority in the Company.

## 1.18 Awareness and Training

1.18.1 The Company shall conduct a POSH awareness session for all Employees **not less than once in each Financial Year**, and shall include the same in the induction of every new Employee.

1.18.2 Members of the Internal Committee shall be provided capacity-building and skill-building programmes not less than once in each Financial Year, in accordance with Section 19(c) of the Act.

1.18.3 A record of attendance at such sessions shall be maintained by HR and shall inform the annual report at clause 1.16.

# ANNEXURE B — FORM OF COMPLAINT

A complaint under clause 1.7 should ordinarily set out:

1. Name, designation, employee code, department, and contact particulars of the Complainant;
2. Name, designation, and department of the Respondent, and the relationship (if any) of the Respondent to the Complainant;
3. Date, time, and place of each incident complained of;
4. A description of each incident, in the Complainant's own words;
5. Names and contact particulars of any witnesses;
6. A list of documents, messages, recordings, or other material relied upon, with copies annexed;
7. Any interim relief sought under clause 1.10;
8. Signature of the Complainant and the date of the complaint.

Six copies are to be submitted in accordance with clause 1.7.3. Assistance in reducing a complaint to writing is available from the Presiding Officer or any Member of the Internal Committee.

# ITEMS PENDING FINALISATION

This Policy is issued and takes effect on the Effective Date without awaiting the items below; clause 1.5.9 governs the interim period. Items 1 to 3 are nevertheless **statutory obligations** and are to be completed **within ninety days of the Effective Date** — the Act requires that the Internal Committee exist and be publicised, and non-constitution attracts the penalty at clause 1.17.

1. **Constitution of the Internal Committee (clause 1.5, Annexure A):** Issue the written order of the Employer nominating the Presiding Officer, not fewer than two Employee Members, and one External Member, ensuring that not less than one-half of the members are women. Record the date of constitution and the three-year term expiry, and notify by circular in the form of Annexure A.
2. **External Member engagement (clauses 1.5.2(c) and 1.5.5):** Identify and engage the External Member, and fix the fees/allowances payable per proceeding in accordance with Rule 4 of the Rules.
3. **Statutory display (clause 1.6.1(b)):** Display the penal consequences of sexual harassment at conspicuous places at the registered office and every other location of the Company. Add the composition and contact particulars of the Internal Committee to that display upon its notification; until then, display the interim complaint channel at clause 1.5.9(b).
4. **Interim complaint channel (clause 1.5.9(b)):** Publish, on the Employee Portal and on the statutory display, the channel for making a complaint pending notification of the Internal Committee, routed so that it is accessible only to the Chief Executive Officer.
5. **Dedicated Internal Committee mailbox:** Upon notification of the Internal Committee, publish a dedicated e-mail address for the Presiding Officer, routed so that it is accessible only to the Internal Committee, and retire the interim channel.
6. **Employee acknowledgement (clause 1.6.2):** Enable acknowledgement of this Policy on the Employee Portal for all active Employees, and add it to the induction checklist.
7. **First awareness session (clause 1.18.1):** Schedule the first organisation-wide POSH awareness session within ninety days of the Effective Date, and the first capacity-building programme for the Internal Committee within ninety days of its notification.
8. **Annual report calendar (clause 1.16):** Diarise submission of the annual report to the District Officer, Chengalpattu District, for each calendar year, and the corresponding disclosure in the Board's Report.

*End of Policy.*
