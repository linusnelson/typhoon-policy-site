## Preamble

This Policy ("**Policy**") forms part of the Human Resources Policy Manual of Typhoon Electronics Solutions ("**the Company**", "**Typhoon Electronics**", "**Employer**") and shall apply to all Employees with effect from the Effective Date stated above. This Policy is issued under the authority of the Chief Executive Officer and supersedes all prior policies, circulars, oral understandings, or established practices on the matters covered herein.

This Policy is intended to be consistent with the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 ("**RPwD Act**"), the constitutional principles of equality, and all other Central and State labour enactments as may apply to the Company from time to time.

The Company reserves the right to amend, modify, or rescind this Policy, or any part thereof, at any time, with or without prior notice. Any such modification shall be communicated through an official circular issued by the Human Resources function or the Chief Executive Officer.

## Scope and Applicability

This Policy shall apply to:

1. All persons employed by Typhoon Electronics on a full-time basis, whether on probation or confirmed;
2. Trainees and apprentices, save to the extent that any specific provision is rendered inapplicable by statute or by the express terms of their engagement;
3. Applicants for employment with the Company, in respect of recruitment-related provisions;
4. Where the context requires, contractors, vendors' personnel, interns, and visitors in their dealings with the Company.

Each individual to whom this Policy applies is hereinafter referred to as an "**Employee**", unless the context requires otherwise.

## Definitions

For the purposes of this Policy, the following terms shall bear the meanings ascribed to them below:

- **"Company"** means Typhoon Electronics Solutions, including its successors and assigns.
- **"Code of Conduct"** means the Typhoon Electronics Code of Conduct, issued separately as part of the HR Policy Manual.
- **"Effective Date"** means 1 April 2026.
- **"Employee"** has the meaning given in the *Scope and Applicability* section above.
- **"HR"** or **"Human Resources"** means the function or person designated by the Company to administer this Policy.
- **"Management"** means the Chief Executive Officer and such other persons as the CEO may designate.
- **"Protected Characteristics"** has the meaning given at clause 1.3.2.
- **"Reasonable Accommodation"** has the meaning given at clause 1.5.2.

## 1.1 Purpose

Typhoon Electronics is committed to providing a workplace free from discrimination and harassment, and to ensuring equality of opportunity in all aspects of employment. This Policy sets out the Company's commitment, the prohibited conduct, and the mechanisms for redress in respect of discrimination and all kinds of harassment.

## 1.2 Scope

This Policy applies to all Employees as defined in the *Scope and Applicability* section of this Policy, and, where the context requires, to applicants for employment and to contractors, vendors' personnel, interns, and visitors in their dealings with the Company.

## 1.3 Equal Opportunity Commitment

1.3.1 The Company shall provide equal opportunity to all Employees and applicants in all matters relating to employment, including (but not limited to) recruitment, terms of employment, remuneration, training, promotion, transfer, discipline, and separation.

1.3.2 No decision affecting employment shall be made on the basis of any of the following **protected characteristics**:

(a) Gender;
(b) Religion;
(c) Caste;
(d) Disability;
(e) Age;
(f) Marital status;
(g) Sexual orientation;
(h) Place of origin;
(i) Pregnancy or maternity status;
(j) Language.

1.3.3 The Company's commitment to equal opportunity is informed by the constitutional principles of equality, the RPwD Act, and the spirit of Indian labour legislation.

## 1.4 Prohibited Conduct

1.4.1 The following are strictly prohibited:

(a) **Discrimination** — any less favourable treatment of a person on any protected ground listed at clause 1.3.2;
(b) **Harassment** — any unwelcome conduct that has the purpose or effect of creating an intimidating, hostile, degrading, humiliating, or offensive environment, save where such conduct constitutes sexual harassment;
(c) **Victimisation or retaliation** — any adverse action against a person for raising a complaint, supporting another's complaint, or participating in an inquiry.

## 1.5 Reasonable Accommodation

1.5.1 The Company shall, to the extent reasonable and practicable, provide accommodation to Employees and applicants with disabilities, having regard to the nature of the role, the workplace environment, and the resources of the Company.

1.5.2 Reasonable accommodation may include, where appropriate:

(a) Modifications to the physical workspace to ensure accessibility;
(b) Provision of assistive technology;
(c) Adjustments to working hours or schedules;
(d) Modification of duties, where consistent with the requirements of the role;
(e) Such other adjustments as may reasonably be required.

1.5.3 An Employee seeking accommodation under this clause may make a written request to HR, which shall be considered in good faith and in consultation with the Employee.

## 1.6 Complaint Procedure

1.6.1 Any Employee who believes they have experienced or witnessed discrimination, non-sexual harassment, or retaliation under this Policy may make a complaint in writing to HR or to the Chief Executive Officer.

1.6.2 The Company shall:

(a) Acknowledge receipt of the complaint promptly;
(b) Investigate the complaint impartially and confidentially;
(c) Provide both the complainant and the respondent a fair opportunity to be heard;
(d) Take appropriate action, including action under the Code of Conduct, where the complaint is substantiated;
(e) Communicate the outcome to the parties.

1.6.3 Complaints shall be treated as confidential to the maximum extent possible, save as required for the proper conduct of the inquiry or by law.
