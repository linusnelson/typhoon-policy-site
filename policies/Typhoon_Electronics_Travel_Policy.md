## Preamble

This Policy ("**Policy**") forms part of the Human Resources Policy Manual of Typhoon Electronics Solutions ("**the Company**", "**Typhoon Electronics**", "**Employer**") and shall apply to all Employees with effect from the Effective Date stated above. This Policy is issued under the authority of the Chief Executive Officer and supersedes all prior policies, circulars, oral understandings, or established practices on the matters covered herein.

The Company reserves the right to amend, modify, or rescind this Policy, or any part thereof, at any time, with or without prior notice. Any such modification shall be communicated through an official circular issued by the Human Resources function or the Chief Executive Officer.

## Scope and Applicability

This Policy shall apply to:

1. All persons employed by Typhoon Electronics on a full-time basis, whether on probation or confirmed;
2. Trainees and apprentices, save to the extent that any specific provision is rendered inapplicable by statute or by the express terms of their engagement;
3. Where expressly stated, contractors, consultants, interns, and visitors authorised to undertake business travel on behalf of the Company.

Each individual to whom this Policy applies is hereinafter referred to as an "**Employee**", unless the context requires otherwise.

## Definitions

For the purposes of this Policy, the following terms shall bear the meanings ascribed to them below:

- **"Company"** means Typhoon Electronics Solutions, including its successors and assigns.
- **"Code of Conduct"** means the Typhoon Electronics Code of Conduct, issued separately as part of the HR Policy Manual.
- **"Effective Date"** means 1 April 2026.
- **"Employee"** has the meaning given in the *Scope and Applicability* section above.
- **"HR"** or **"Human Resources"** means the function or person designated by the Company to administer this Policy.
- **"Management"** means the Chief Executive Officer and such other persons as the CEO may designate.
- **"Reporting Manager"** means the immediate supervisor of an Employee as recorded in the Company's records.

## 1.1 Purpose

This Policy governs business travel undertaken by Employees on behalf of the Company, including pre-approval, entitlements, and the reimbursement of related expenses.

## 1.2 Scope

This Policy applies to all Employees authorised to undertake business travel on behalf of the Company, whether domestic or international.

## 1.3 Pre-Approval

1.3.1 No Employee shall undertake business travel without prior written approval from the Reporting Manager and the Chief Executive Officer (or such authority as the CEO may designate).

1.3.2 The request for approval shall be submitted by email and shall include:

(a) Purpose and business justification of the travel;
(b) Destination, dates of departure and return;
(c) Mode of travel (flight, train, road) and class proposed;
(d) Accommodation proposed;
(e) An estimated budget covering travel, stay, food, and incidentals.

1.3.3 Travel shall be undertaken strictly in accordance with the approved estimate. Any material deviation from the approved estimate shall require fresh approval before incurring the additional expense.

## 1.4 Entitlements

1.4.1 Class of travel, accommodation category, and other entitlements shall be as per the **approved travel estimate** for each trip, having regard to the nature and purpose of the travel, the destination, and the Employee's role.

1.4.2 The Company shall publish a detailed grade-wise entitlement matrix at a later stage. Until such matrix is published, entitlements shall be determined on a per-trip basis through the pre-approval process at clause 1.3.

## 1.5 Reimbursement Model

1.5.1 The Company shall reimburse business travel expenses on the following basis:

| Head of Expense | Basis of Reimbursement |
|---|---|
| Travel (flight, train, road) | Actuals, against original tickets/bills |
| Accommodation (Stay) | Actuals, against original hotel invoice |
| Local Transport (cabs, autos) | Actuals, against receipts or trip records |
| Food / Meals | Fixed daily allowance of ₹ 450 per person per day, in lieu of bills |
| Other approved expenses | Actuals, against original bills |

1.5.2 The food allowance at ₹ 450 per person per day shall apply uniformly and shall not require submission of bills. Any expenditure on food in excess of this allowance shall be borne by the Employee.

1.5.3 The Company shall reimburse expenses on Stay, Food, and Transport. Heads not expressly listed (such as personal entertainment, alcoholic beverages, mini-bar charges, laundry for short trips, and similar items) shall not be reimbursable.

## 1.6 Submission of Documents

1.6.1 All claims for reimbursement shall be supported by original receipts, invoices, or tickets, except where the head of expense is reimbursed on a per-diem basis under clause 1.5.

1.6.2 Submission of any forged, altered, or duplicated bill shall be treated as serious misconduct and shall attract action under the Code of Conduct.

## 1.7 Claim Submission Timeline

1.7.1 An Employee shall submit the travel claim, together with all supporting documents, within ten (10) calendar days of the date of return from travel.

1.7.2 Claims received after the above timeline may be rejected at the discretion of HR or Management, save where the delay is justified.

## 1.8 Reimbursement Payment

1.8.1 Approved travel reimbursements shall be credited to the Employee along with the subsequent salary cycle following approval of the claim.

1.8.2 In cases of genuine emergency or financial hardship, an Employee may make a written request for expedited processing, which shall be considered by HR and Management on a case-by-case basis.

## 1.9 Travel Advances

1.9.1 Travel advances shall not ordinarily be issued.

1.9.2 In cases of emergency or where the nature of travel reasonably warrants an advance (e.g., international travel, extended duration travel), an Employee may, on written request supported by the approved estimate, be granted an advance at the discretion of Management.

1.9.3 The advance shall be reconciled against actual expenses upon the Employee's return, and any unspent balance shall be remitted to the Company without delay.
