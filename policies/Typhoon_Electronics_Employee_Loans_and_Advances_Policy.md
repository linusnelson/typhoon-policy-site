## Preamble

This Policy ("**Policy**") forms part of the Human Resources Policy Manual of Typhoon Electronics Solutions ("**the Company**", "**Typhoon Electronics**", "**Employer**") and shall apply to all Employees with effect from the Effective Date stated above. This Policy is issued under the authority of the Chief Executive Officer and supersedes all prior policies, circulars, oral understandings, or established practices on the matters covered herein.

The Company reserves the right to amend, modify, or rescind this Policy, or any part thereof, at any time, with or without prior notice. Any such modification shall be communicated through an official circular issued by the Human Resources function or the Chief Executive Officer.

## Scope and Applicability

This Policy shall apply to:

1. All persons employed by Typhoon Electronics on a full-time basis, whether on probation or confirmed, subject to the eligibility conditions at clause 1.4;
2. Trainees and apprentices only where expressly approved by Management on a case-by-case basis.

Each individual to whom this Policy applies is hereinafter referred to as an "**Employee**", unless the context requires otherwise.

## Definitions

For the purposes of this Policy, the following terms shall bear the meanings ascribed to them below:

- **"Loan/Advance"** means an interest-free loan or salary advance granted by the Company to an Employee under this Policy, recoverable by deduction from salary in EMIs. In the books of the Company such amounts are carried under *Loans and Advances*.
- **"Company"** means Typhoon Electronics Solutions, including its successors and assigns.
- **"Code of Conduct"** means the Typhoon Electronics Code of Conduct, issued separately as part of the HR Policy Manual.
- **"Declared EMIs"** means the monthly repayment obligations on the Employee's existing external borrowings (home, vehicle, personal, or other loans) as declared by the Employee in the Employee Portal.
- **"Declared Salary"** means the gross monthly salary declared by the Employee at the time of application, subject to verification by Management against Company records.
- **"Effective Date"** means 1 April 2026.
- **"EMI"** means one equated monthly instalment of a Loan/Advance, recovered by deduction from salary.
- **"Employee"** has the meaning given in the *Scope and Applicability* section above.
- **"Employee Portal"** means the Company's internal web and mobile platform through which Loan/Advance requests are submitted, checked for eligibility, approved, and tracked, and which constitutes the Company's system of record.
- **"HR"** or **"Human Resources"** means the function or person designated by the Company to administer this Policy.
- **"Management"** means the Chief Executive Officer and such other persons as the CEO may designate.
- **"Outstanding Balance"** means the sum of all EMIs of a Loan/Advance that remain scheduled and unrecovered.
- **"Repayment Capacity"** has the meaning given in clause 1.5.
- **"Security Cheques"** means the three signed cheque leaves deposited by the Employee under clause 1.7.4 as security for repayment of a Loan/Advance.

## 1.1 Purpose

This Policy governs the grant, disbursement, and recovery of interest-free loans and salary advances extended by the Company to Employees to assist with genuine personal financial needs, while safeguarding the Company's funds through a transparent eligibility formula, defined limits, approval controls, and structured recovery.

## 1.2 Scope

This Policy applies to all Loans/Advances requested on or after the Effective Date. It does not apply to travel advances, which are governed exclusively by clause 1.9 of the Typhoon Electronics Travel Policy, nor to statutory dues, reimbursements, or any other payment obligation of the Company.

## 1.3 Nature of the Facility

1.3.1 A Loan/Advance is a **discretionary facility** and not a term or condition of employment. No Employee has an entitlement to a Loan/Advance, and the grant of one on any occasion shall not create a precedent or expectation for any future occasion.

1.3.2 Loans/Advances are **interest-free**.

1.3.3 The Company may decline any request without assigning reasons, and may suspend or withdraw the facility generally, or in respect of any Employee, at any time.

## 1.4 Eligibility

1.4.1 An Employee shall be eligible to apply only if, on the date of application:

(a) the Employee is on the active rolls of the Company and is not serving a notice period, and is not suspended or subject to a pending disciplinary proceeding;
(b) the Employee has completed the minimum period of continuous service configured for the Employee's department in the Employee Portal;
(c) the Employee has no other Loan/Advance that is pending, approved, or under repayment, save where the configured policy expressly permits concurrent facilities;
(d) any cooling-off period configured in the Employee Portal since the closure of the Employee's previous Loan/Advance has elapsed; and
(e) the Employee has **read and digitally signed the current version of this Policy** in the Employee Portal. The Portal shall not accept an application from an Employee who has not signed, and a fresh signature shall be required whenever an amended version of this Policy is published with re-acknowledgement required.

1.4.2 **Declarations.** At or before application the Employee shall declare in the Employee Portal:

(a) their **Declared Salary**; and
(b) their **Declared EMIs** — every existing external loan or EMI obligation, with the lender and monthly amount.

1.4.3 Declarations are made on the Employee's own responsibility. Management may require documentary proof (payslip, loan statements) and shall verify the Declared Salary against Company records at approval. A false or incomplete declaration is misconduct under clause 1.12.

## 1.5 Repayment Capacity and Amount Limits

1.5.1 The Employee's **Repayment Capacity** — the maximum EMI a new Loan/Advance may carry — is computed as:

> **Repayment Capacity = (Declared Salary − Declared EMIs) × the Configured Percentage − EMIs of any open Company Loans/Advances**

1.5.2 The **Configured Percentage** is **fifty per cent (50%)**, applied uniformly across the organisation. This corresponds to the statutory ceiling on deductions from wages — fifty per cent (50%) of wages in a wage period under Section 7(3) of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936 and Section 18(3) of the Code on Wages, 2019. Accordingly, the maximum EMI a new Loan/Advance may carry is **50% of (Declared Salary − Declared EMIs)**, less the EMIs of any open Company Loans/Advances.

1.5.3 The maximum tenure of a Loan/Advance is **ten (10) months**. The maximum amount of a Loan/Advance shall be the **lowest of**:

(a) the fixed monetary ceiling configured in the Employee Portal, which applies organisation-wide;
(b) the configured multiple of the Declared Salary, where such a multiple is configured; and
(c) the Repayment Capacity multiplied by the maximum tenure of ten (10) months.

1.5.4 The applicable limits and the resulting eligibility for the amount and tenure requested are **displayed to the Employee in the Employee Portal at the time of application**, before submission.

## 1.6 Application Procedure

1.6.1 An application shall be submitted **only through the Employee Portal**, stating:

(a) the Declared Salary;
(b) the amount requested;
(c) the number of repayment months proposed (any tenure up to the maximum of ten (10) months, at the Employee's choice); and
(d) the reason for the request, where the configured policy requires a reason.

1.6.2 The Employee Portal computes the Repayment Capacity from the declarations and validates the application before submission; an application whose EMI exceeds the Repayment Capacity, or which otherwise fails the configured conditions, cannot be submitted. The Portal indicates the minimum tenure at which the requested amount would become eligible.

1.6.3 The Declared Salary and Declared EMIs are recorded on the application as made, and form part of the audit record of the request.

1.6.4 An Employee may cancel their own application at any time before it is approved.

## 1.7 Approval

1.7.1 Approval is reserved **exclusively to Management** (or an administrator expressly designated by Management). Reporting Managers shall not approve Loans/Advances.

1.7.2 The approving authority shall review, without limitation: the Declared Salary **as against the salary in Company records**, the Declared EMIs, the resulting Repayment Capacity, the proposed EMI, the Employee's tenure, any other open or recently closed facilities, and the operational and financial circumstances of the Company.

1.7.3 The decision, together with any remarks of the approving authority, shall be communicated to the Employee through the Employee Portal.

1.7.4 **Security Cheques.** As a condition of approval, the Employee shall deposit with the Company **three (3) signed cheque leaves** drawn on the Employee's salary bank account, as security for repayment of the Loan/Advance ("**Security Cheques**"). No Loan/Advance shall be disbursed until the Security Cheques have been received and recorded by HR. Upon closure of the Loan/Advance under clause 1.9.5, any unused Security Cheques shall be returned to the Employee or destroyed with the Employee's consent, and the return or destruction shall be recorded.

## 1.8 Disbursement

1.8.1 An approved Loan/Advance shall be disbursed by bank transfer or through payroll, as determined by the Company, and the disbursement shall be recorded in the Employee Portal.

1.8.2 Upon disbursement, the Employee Portal shall generate a repayment schedule of equal monthly EMIs commencing from the first deduction month recorded at disbursement (by default, the month following disbursement). Where the amount does not divide equally, the final EMI shall absorb the rounding difference.

1.8.3 An approved but undisbursed Loan/Advance may be cancelled by Management or, upon written request, at the instance of the Employee.

## 1.9 Repayment

1.9.1 Repayment shall be effected by **deduction from the Employee's monthly salary** in accordance with the repayment schedule, in compliance with applicable law on permissible deductions from wages.

1.9.2 An Employee may, at any time, repay the entire Outstanding Balance early, in which event the remaining EMIs shall be marked settled and the facility closed.

1.9.3 The repayment schedule shall **not be rescheduled after disbursement**. In exceptional circumstances, Management may in writing cancel the remaining schedule and record a fresh arrangement.

1.9.4 Management may, in writing and at its sole discretion, waive one or more EMIs. A waiver of any EMI shall not constitute a waiver of any other EMI or of any other right of the Company.

1.9.5 A Loan/Advance shall stand closed when no EMI remains scheduled, whether by recovery, early settlement, or written waiver.

## 1.10 Recovery on Cessation of Employment

1.10.1 Upon resignation, retirement, termination, or death in service of an Employee with an Outstanding Balance, the entire Outstanding Balance shall become immediately due and shall be recovered from any salary and other amounts payable to the Employee at cessation, to the maximum extent permitted by law. The Company does not operate a separate full-and-final settlement procedure; recovery under this clause is effected directly against such amounts.

1.10.2 Where the amounts recoverable under clause 1.10.1 are insufficient to extinguish the Outstanding Balance, the Employee (or the Employee's estate, as the case may be) shall remit the shortfall to the Company before issuance of the relieving letter and final clearance, failing which the Company may present one or more of the Security Cheques deposited under clause 1.7.4 toward recovery of the shortfall, without prejudice to any other remedy available to the Company in law. Management may in writing determine otherwise in any individual case.

1.10.3 No Employee with an Outstanding Balance shall be deactivated from the Company's records without the balance first being addressed under this clause.

## 1.11 Records and Confidentiality

1.11.1 The Employee Portal constitutes the Company's system of record for all Loans/Advances, including declarations, applications, eligibility computations, approvals, disbursements, repayment schedules, recoveries, waivers, and closures.

1.11.2 Salary, EMI declarations, and Loan/Advance information are confidential and shall be accessible only to the Employee concerned and to Management or its designated administrators. Such information shall not be displayed to, or shared with, other Employees.

## 1.12 Misuse and Misconduct

1.12.1 Submission of a false or misleading Declared Salary, concealment or understatement of existing EMIs, or any attempt to circumvent the limits, validations, or approval controls of this Policy or of the Employee Portal, shall be treated as serious misconduct and shall attract action under the Code of Conduct, without prejudice to the Company's right to recall the Loan/Advance and recover it in full forthwith.

## 1.13 Taxes

1.13.1 Under Rule 3(7)(i) of the Income-tax Rules, 1962, an interest-free or concessional loan from an employer may constitute a **taxable perquisite** in the hands of the Employee (generally where the aggregate outstanding exceeds ₹20,000 and the loan is not for medical treatment of specified diseases). Any tax arising from a Loan/Advance under this Policy shall be borne by the Employee and shall be dealt with in accordance with applicable law, including through tax deduction at source where required.
