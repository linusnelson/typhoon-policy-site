## Preamble

This Code of Conduct ("**Code**") forms part of the Human Resources Policy Manual of Typhoon Electronics Solutions ("**the Company**", "**Typhoon Electronics**", "**Employer**") and shall apply to all Employees with effect from the Effective Date stated above. This Code is issued under the authority of the Chief Executive Officer and supersedes all prior policies, circulars, oral understandings, or established practices on the matters covered herein.

Adherence to this Code is a condition of employment with Typhoon Electronics.

The Company reserves the right to amend, modify, or rescind this Code, or any part thereof, at any time, with or without prior notice. Any such modification shall be communicated through an official circular issued by the Human Resources function or the Chief Executive Officer.

## Scope and Applicability

This Code shall apply to:

1. All persons employed by Typhoon Electronics on a full-time basis, whether on probation or confirmed;
2. Trainees and apprentices, save to the extent that any specific provision is rendered inapplicable by statute or by the express terms of their engagement;
3. Where expressly stated, contractors, consultants, interns, and visitors, in relation to those provisions that govern their conduct on Company premises or in connection with Company business.

Each individual to whom this Code applies is hereinafter referred to as an "**Employee**", unless the context requires otherwise.

## Definitions

For the purposes of this Code, the following terms shall bear the meanings ascribed to them below:

- **"Company"** means Typhoon Electronics Solutions, including its successors and assigns.
- **"Confidential Information"** has the meaning given at clause 1.5.2.
- **"Effective Date"** means 1 June 2026.
- **"Employee"** has the meaning given in the *Scope and Applicability* section above.
- **"Equal Opportunity Policy"** means the Typhoon Electronics Equal Opportunity & Anti-Discrimination Policy, issued separately as part of the HR Policy Manual.
- **"HR"** or **"Human Resources"** means the function or person designated by the Company to administer this Code.
- **"IT Assets"** has the meaning given at clause 1.6.1.
- **"Management"** means the Chief Executive Officer and such other persons as the CEO may designate.
- **"Reporting Manager"** means the immediate supervisor of an Employee as recorded in the Company's records.

## 1.1 Purpose

This Code of Conduct ("**Code**") sets forth the standards of professional and ethical behaviour expected of every Employee of Typhoon Electronics. Adherence to the Code is a condition of employment.

## 1.2 Core Values

The Company is built on, and committed to upholding, the following core values, which every Employee is expected to embody:

1. **Integrity** — honesty, transparency, and consistency between word and deed in all dealings.
2. **Respect** — treating colleagues, clients, vendors, and visitors with dignity and courtesy.
3. **Accountability** — owning one's responsibilities, decisions, and their consequences.
4. **Customer Focus** — placing the needs and trust of the customer at the centre of every action.
5. **Innovation** — seeking better ways to do things, and welcoming considered change.
6. **Teamwork** — collaborating constructively, sharing knowledge, and supporting one another.
7. **Safety** — protecting oneself, colleagues, and Company property from harm.
8. **Quality** — delivering work that meets professional standards and stands the test of scrutiny.

## 1.3 Conflict of Interest

1.3.1 Every Employee shall act in the best interests of the Company and shall avoid any situation in which personal interests, whether financial or otherwise, conflict, or may reasonably be perceived to conflict, with the interests of the Company.

1.3.2 Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, Employees shall disclose to HR, in writing and at the earliest opportunity, any of the following:

(a) Direct or indirect financial interest in any vendor, customer, competitor, or business partner of the Company;
(b) Engagement of a relative as a vendor, contractor, or consultant of the Company;
(c) Personal relationships (familial or romantic) with a Reporting Manager or a direct report;
(d) Any outside engagement that may give rise to a conflict (see clause 1.9 on Moonlighting).

1.3.3 The Company shall determine, in consultation with the Employee, appropriate steps to manage any disclosed conflict.

## 1.4 Gifts and Anti-Bribery

1.4.1 No Employee shall offer, give, solicit, or accept, directly or indirectly, any bribe, kickback, illegal gratification, or other improper inducement, whether in cash or in kind, in connection with the business of the Company.

1.4.2 Modest gifts of nominal value (such as customary festival sweets, branded merchandise, or stationery) given or received in the ordinary course of business may be acceptable, provided they:

(a) Do not exceed a value of ₹ 2,500 per occasion;
(b) Are not in the form of cash or cash equivalents;
(c) Are not solicited;
(d) Could not reasonably be perceived to influence business decisions.

1.4.3 Gifts exceeding the threshold at clause 1.4.2 shall be declared to HR and shall not be retained without written approval.

1.4.4 This clause shall be read in conjunction with the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, and all other applicable anti-corruption legislation.

## 1.5 Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

1.5.1 Every Employee shall maintain in strict confidence all Confidential Information of the Company, of its clients, and of its business partners, both during and after the term of employment.

1.5.2 "**Confidential Information**" includes, without limitation: technical designs and specifications; software code; client lists, pricing, and contracts; financial information; business plans and strategies; trade secrets; vendor information; personnel information; and any information marked as confidential or which a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

1.5.3 All intellectual property created by an Employee in the course of, or in connection with, employment with the Company — including inventions, designs, software, written materials, processes, and improvements — shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company. The Employee shall execute such assignments, declarations, and other documents as the Company may reasonably require to vest such intellectual property in the Company.

1.5.4 Upon separation from the Company, the Employee shall return all Confidential Information and intellectual property in any form (physical, digital, or otherwise) without retaining any copies.

## 1.6 IT and Acceptable Use

1.6.1 Company-issued computers, network access, email accounts, software licences, and other information technology assets ("**IT Assets**") shall be used primarily for legitimate business purposes. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work or violate this Code.

1.6.2 Employees shall not:

(a) Install unauthorised software on Company IT Assets;
(b) Access, download, distribute, or store unlawful, obscene, defamatory, or infringing material;
(c) Attempt to bypass security controls or access systems or data without authorisation;
(d) Share login credentials with any other person;
(e) Use Company IT Assets to conduct any business unrelated to the Company;
(f) Disable or tamper with security software, monitoring tools, or audit logs.

1.6.3 The Company reserves the right to monitor, inspect, and audit the use of Company IT Assets, including email and internet usage, in accordance with applicable law. Employees shall have no expectation of privacy in respect of communications or data on Company IT Assets.

## 1.7 Social Media

1.7.1 Employees shall exercise discretion and good judgement in their use of social media, whether personal or professional.

1.7.2 An Employee shall not, on any social media platform:

(a) Disclose Confidential Information of the Company or its clients;
(b) Make statements that bring, or are likely to bring, the Company, its Employees, clients, or business partners into disrepute;
(c) Impersonate the Company or speak on its behalf without authorisation;
(d) Post discriminatory, harassing, defamatory, obscene, or threatening content directed at any person, including colleagues.

1.7.3 Where an Employee identifies their association with the Company on a personal social media profile, the Employee shall make it clear that views expressed are personal and do not represent the views of the Company.

## 1.8 Substance / Alcohol at the Workplace

1.8.1 The consumption of alcoholic beverages, narcotic drugs, or psychotropic substances on Company premises or during working hours is strictly prohibited.

1.8.2 No Employee shall report to work, or remain at work, in a state of intoxication or under the influence of any substance that impairs the Employee's ability to perform duties safely and competently.

1.8.3 Consumption of alcohol at Company-authorised events (such as celebrations, off-sites, or client functions) is permitted in moderation, and Employees shall continue to be bound by the standards of conduct in this Code at such events.

## 1.9 Moonlighting / Outside Employment

1.9.1 An Employee shall not, during the term of employment with the Company, engage in any other employment, business, profession, consultancy, or paid engagement, whether full-time or part-time, without the prior written approval of the Company.

1.9.2 An application for such approval shall disclose the nature, duration, and remuneration of the proposed outside engagement, and shall confirm that it does not, and will not, conflict with the Employee's duties to the Company, compete with the Company, or involve the use of Company resources or Confidential Information.

1.9.3 Pro bono, academic, or charitable activities that do not impair the Employee's performance and do not give rise to a conflict shall not require approval but should be disclosed for transparency.

## 1.10 Workplace Behaviour and Harassment

1.10.1 Every Employee shall conduct themselves with courtesy, dignity, and professionalism in all interactions with colleagues, clients, vendors, and visitors.

1.10.2 The following conduct is strictly prohibited:

(a) Bullying, intimidation, or coercion of any colleague;
(b) Verbal abuse, threats, or use of offensive language;
(c) Physical violence or any threat thereof;
(d) Sexual harassment;
(e) Discrimination on any protected ground (see the Equal Opportunity Policy);
(f) Retaliation against any person who has raised a complaint or supported an investigation.

1.10.3 Any Employee who believes they have witnessed or experienced prohibited conduct under this clause is encouraged to report it to HR.

## 1.11 Punctuality and Attendance Conduct

1.11.1 Every Employee shall observe the working hours stipulated under the Typhoon Electronics Attendance & Leave Policy and shall be punctual in commencing work, attending meetings, and meeting deadlines.

1.11.2 Habitual absenteeism, unauthorised absence, or repeated late arrival shall constitute misconduct and shall be addressed under the disciplinary procedure at clause 1.13.

## 1.12 Use of Company Property

1.12.1 Company property, including IT Assets, office equipment, vehicles, premises, and supplies, shall be used solely for legitimate business purposes and shall be maintained with reasonable care.

1.12.2 No Employee shall remove Company property from the premises without authorisation, nor use Company property for personal gain or for the benefit of any third party.

1.12.3 Upon separation, the Employee shall return all Company property in good working condition, subject to normal wear and tear.

## 1.13 Disciplinary Procedure

1.13.1 Where an Employee is alleged to have committed misconduct or breached this Code or any other policy of the Company, the following procedure shall be observed.

1.13.2 **Stages of Disciplinary Action.** The Company shall, depending on the gravity and the recurrence of the conduct, take action through one or more of the following stages:

| Stage | Description |
|---|---|
| Stage 1 | **Verbal Warning** — issued by the Reporting Manager and recorded in the Employee's file by HR |
| Stage 2 | **Written Warning** — issued by HR or Management, requiring acknowledgement by the Employee |
| Stage 3 | **Final Written Warning** — issued by Management, with explicit notice that further violation may lead to termination |
| Stage 4 | **Suspension or Termination** — imposed by Management following due inquiry |

1.13.3 **Right to Be Heard.** Before any action at Stage 2 or higher is taken, the Employee shall:

(a) Be issued a show-cause notice in writing setting out the alleged misconduct;
(b) Be given a reasonable opportunity, of not less than three (3) Working Days, to submit a written response;
(c) Where the matter is contested or its facts are not clear, be given an opportunity to be heard in person.

1.13.4 **Gross Misconduct.** Notwithstanding the staged approach at clause 1.13.2, conduct constituting gross misconduct (including but not limited to theft, fraud, falsification of records, physical violence, sexual harassment, serious breach of confidentiality, or insubordination) may attract direct action at Stage 4, subject to the right to be heard under clause 1.13.3.

1.13.5 **Suspension Pending Inquiry.** Where the Company is of the view that the continued presence of the Employee may prejudice an investigation or pose a risk, the Employee may be placed on suspension pending inquiry. Suspension shall not by itself be regarded as a finding of guilt.

1.13.6 **Appeal.** An Employee aggrieved by any disciplinary action at Stage 2 or higher may, within seven (7) Working Days of receiving notice of the action, prefer a written appeal to the Chief Executive Officer (or, where the original action was taken by the CEO, to a senior person nominated by the CEO). The decision on appeal shall be final and binding.
