## Preamble

This Policy ("**Policy**") forms part of the Human Resources Policy Manual of Typhoon Electronics Solutions ("**the Company**", "**Typhoon Electronics**", "**Employer**") and shall apply to all Employees with effect from the Effective Date stated above. This Policy is issued under the authority of the Chief Executive Officer and supersedes all prior policies, circulars, oral understandings, or established practices on the matters covered herein.

The Company reserves the right to amend, modify, or rescind this Policy, or any part thereof, at any time, with or without prior notice. Any such modification shall be communicated through an official circular issued by the Human Resources function or the Chief Executive Officer.

## Scope and Applicability

This Policy shall apply to:

1. All persons employed by Typhoon Electronics on a full-time basis, whether on probation or confirmed;
2. Trainees and apprentices, save to the extent that any specific provision is rendered inapplicable by statute or by the express terms of their engagement;
3. Where expressly stated, contractors, consultants, and interns present on Company premises or representing the Company.

Each individual to whom this Policy applies is hereinafter referred to as an "**Employee**", unless the context requires otherwise.

## Definitions

For the purposes of this Policy, the following terms shall bear the meanings ascribed to them below:

- **"Company"** means Typhoon Electronics Solutions, including its successors and assigns.
- **"Code of Conduct"** means the Typhoon Electronics Code of Conduct, issued separately as part of the HR Policy Manual.
- **"Effective Date"** means 1 June 2026.
- **"Employee"** has the meaning given in the *Scope and Applicability* section above.
- **"HR"** or **"Human Resources"** means the function or person designated by the Company to administer this Policy.
- **"Management"** means the Chief Executive Officer and such other persons as the CEO may designate.
- **"Working Day"** means Monday through Friday between 9:30 AM and 6:30 PM, and Saturday between 9:30 AM and 1:00 PM, excluding declared public holidays.

## 1.1 Purpose

This Policy prescribes the standards of attire to be observed by Employees, to maintain a professional appearance consistent with the Company's image and the expectations of clients and visitors.

## 1.2 Standard Dress Code

1.2.1 The dress code applicable on Working Days shall be as follows:

| Day | Dress Code |
|---|---|
| Monday – Friday | **Formal attire**, with closed-toe formal shoes mandatory |
| Saturday | **Business Casual**, including jeans and polo t-shirts |

1.2.2 **Formal Attire** means, without limitation:

(a) For men: full-sleeve formal shirt, formal trousers, and formal closed-toe shoes; tie and blazer optional but encouraged for senior or client-facing roles.
(b) For women: formal trousers/skirt with formal shirt or blouse, formal Indian wear (saree, salwar-kameez, or equivalent), and formal footwear.

1.2.3 **Business Casual** means, without limitation, clean and presentable clothing including collared shirts, polo t-shirts, jeans (in good condition, without rips or tears), and casual footwear. Sportswear, sleeveless tops, shorts, slippers, and flip-flops are not permitted.

## 1.3 Client Meetings and Visitor Days

1.3.1 Formal attire as defined at clause 1.2.2 shall be mandatory for all client meetings, regardless of the day of the week, unless the client's own dress code or the nature of the meeting indicates otherwise.

1.3.2 Where a client visit or external visitor is scheduled on a Saturday, Employees who are likely to interact with the client or visitor shall observe formal attire in place of the Saturday business casual standard.

## 1.4 Grooming and Hygiene

Employees shall maintain a clean and well-groomed appearance at all times. Personal hygiene, neatness of clothing, and general presentability shall be considered part of the dress code under this Policy.

## 1.5 Non-Compliance

1.5.1 An Employee found in non-compliance with this Policy may be required to return home and change, with the absence treated as half-day Casual Leave or Loss of Pay, as applicable.

1.5.2 Repeated non-compliance shall be treated as misconduct and shall attract action under the Code of Conduct.
